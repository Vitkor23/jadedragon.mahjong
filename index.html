Privacy Policy — Jade Dragon Mahjong

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Jade Dragon Mahjong ("the game") is a single-player offline puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what data is processed when you play.

Data we store

Your game progress (levels, stars, boosters, settings and chosen language) is stored only on your device. We do not run our own servers and do not receive this data. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the AdMob SDK may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interaction data. This is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy (see also How Google uses information from apps that use its services).

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Players in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and can change their choice later.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Security

Data sent by the AdMob SDK is encrypted in transit.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
